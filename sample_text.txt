I N D E T A I L
Your Itinerary, Day By Day
DAY 1
Departure from Delhi
Departure from Delhi — exact time and pick-up point will be shared with travelers in advance.
We begin our overnight journey to Jibhi, a tiny hamlet nestled between lush green forests in the
Tirthan Valley of Himachal Pradesh.
DAY 2
Explore the Hidden Gems of Jibhi
Morning: Check in to your hotel and relax. Afterwards, head out for a local sightseeing tour to
the soul-stirring Jibhi Waterfall, followed by a trek to the nearby settlement known as "Mini
Thailand." Explore the local market and enjoy some cafe hopping.
Evening: Gather around the bonfire and enjoy dinner, followed by an overnight stay in Jibhi.
DAY 3
Jalori Pass & Raghupur Fort / Serolsar Lake Trek
Morning: After an early breakfast, head towards Jalori Pass. Choose between two trekking
options — the Raghupur Fort Trek or the Serolsar Lake Trek.
Evening: Dinner under the starry sky, followed by a return to your room for the night.
DAY 4
Chhoie Waterfall & Departure
Morning: Breakfast followed by check-out formalities at the accommodation. A stop will be made
at Gushaini Village for exploring, followed by a trek to the scenic Chhoie Waterfall.
Departure: After the visit to Chhoie Waterfall, board the vehicle for the journey back to Delhi,
arriving the following morning.
